## French Door Refrigerator FAQs

### What is a French door refrigerator?

This refrigerator style features two side-by-side doors on top and a pull-out freezer below. Known for wide shelving and easy access, it’s a great option for families and those who entertain frequently.

### What features should I look for in a French door refrigerator?

Start by considering capacity, dimensions, and whether you want counter-depth or full-depth.

State-of-the-art models often include features like water dispensers, ice makers, and customizable storage. For large households, look for organizational features like adjustable shelves, split shelving, door-in-door access, climate control compartments, air filtration systems, and rapid cooling and freezing functions.

To monitor and adjust your refrigerator settings remotely, look for models with built-in WiFi.

### What size French door refrigerator do I need?

The ideal size for your French door refrigerator depends on the space you have and the amount of storage you need. Our French door refrigerators range between 13 and 29 cu. ft. capacity. The higher the capacity, the more storage you have.

However, you also need to consider the external dimensions. Measure the width, height, and depth available and make sure there’s enough room for ventilation and door-opening without obstruction. You also need to determine whether you want a counter-depth model for a built-in look or a standard-depth refrigerator for extra storage.

### Are French door refrigerators energy efficient?

GE refrigerators are designed to be highly functional and energy efficient. We also have a range of ENERGY STAR® certified refrigerators. These models meet the strict guidelines set by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and use less energy than standard refrigerators. This lessens the impact on the environment as well as your utility bills.
