## Electric Cooktop FAQs: What to Know Before You Buy

### What is an electric cooktop and how does it work?

Electric cooktops are flat surfaces with burners for boiling, frying, searing, griddling, and more.

There are two main types: radiant and Induction. Radiant cooktops use electricity to warm up heating elements beneath the cooktop, whereas induction cooktops use magnetic fields to heat the cookware, not the cooktop itself.

### What features should I look for in an electric cooktop?

For ease of use, look for electric cooktop features like power boil, digital touch controls, and precision temperature settings. Syncburners are also versatile, allowing you to control two elements simultaneously for larger cookware.

We also have models with built-in WiFi so you can monitor burners from your smartphone. And, for easy maintenance, look for a scratch-resistant smoothtop cooktop you can wipe down in seconds.

### What are the advantages of an electric cooktop over gas?

Electric cooktops offer several advantages, particularly with cleaning, efficiency, and convenience.

Electric models have smooth and easy-to-wipe surfaces. They’re easy to install and provide even heat with precision. They’re flame-free, which makes them safer. And, with no indoor emissions, they can be better for those with asthma, allergies, or limited ventilation.

### Are electric cooktops easy to clean?

Yes, electric cooktops are very easy to clean.

Radiant smoothtop and induction cooktops can be cleaned with warm water and a damp cloth or sponge. Use non-abrasive materials and make sure the cooktop has cooled down completely before cleaning.

For stubborn stains, you can also use a cooktop cleaner—just make sure it’s designed for the material (glass, ceramic, etc). Then, dry and buff your cooktop with a clean microfiber cloth.
