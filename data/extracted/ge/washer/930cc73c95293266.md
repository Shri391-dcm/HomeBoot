## Smart GE Dishwashers

With a GE Profile Smart Dishwasher and the SmartHQ app, you can monitor cycle time and status, remotely lock and unlock controls, and automatically reorder detergent. Plus, you'll get alerts if anything prevents a less-than-perfect wash when the load is finished.

GE Appliances dishwashers are available in a range of styles and finishes integrate with your lifestyle. Our smart dishwashers provide cycle monitoring and notifications via our Smart HQ app and your mobile device. We offer built-in dishwashers with front controls or hidden top controls for a sleek, smooth look. Many of our dishwashers feature Dry Boost™ to get hard-to-dry items up to 3 times drier than regular heated cycles. Exclusive bottle jets and hard food disposers provide ultimate cleaning power for sparkling dishes. We also have a line of portable dishwashers for kitchens without dishwasher space—these can be easily wheeled to the sink and hooked up quickly for a fast, easy way to get your dishes clean.
