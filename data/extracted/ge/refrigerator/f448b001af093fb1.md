## How it Works

###  Take Control

 

 Try out the easy-to-use controls of the UltraFast Combo Laundry Machine in our interactive demo.

###  2-in-1 Washer Dryer

 

 Create your own laundry space in a closet, kitchen, or anywhere you have a 120V outlet and water hookup. It’s an all-in-one unit, so it takes up less space while saving you time and energy. Just load your clothes and remove them when they’re clean and dry.

###  Fast Airflow Drying System 

 

 High-speed, recirculated air gently dries even large loads and heavy jeans without high heat. You can wash and dry everything including delicates without needing to hang dry. Don’t be surprised when you take clothes out if at first, they don’t feel warm and dry—just shake them out and they’ll feel dry in seconds.
