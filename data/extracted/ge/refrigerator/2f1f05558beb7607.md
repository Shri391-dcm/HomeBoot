## Dryer FAQs: Find the Right Dryer for Your Home

### What types of dryers are available?

GE Appliances offers three main dryer categories: electric, gas and stackable dryers. These are available in front-loading, top-loading, and portable varieties.

You can use our filters to narrow your options by type as well as factors like capacity, price, and special features.

### What features should I look for in a dryer?

The main things to consider when buying a dryer are capacity and size. Our models range from 3.6 to 7.8 cu. ft. If you’re short on space, consider a stackable dryer to utilize the space above your washer.

State-of-the-art dryers have other useful features, including steam sanitize, wrinkle care, damp alert, reduced static, and built-in WiFi that allows you to manage your laundry from your smartphone.

### Should I choose a gas or electric model?

Whether you choose a gas or electric dryer will mainly depend on your budget and your hookups at home.

Gas dryers need a gas line and a plumber for installation, making the upfront cost higher. Electric dryers are cheaper upfront, but they take longer to dry and often cost more to run.

For more information, check out our guide on the pros and cons of gas and electric dryers.

### What dryer size do I need for my home?

Your dryer size depends on the space available and the amount of laundry you do.

As a rough guide, a 3.5–5.0 cu. ft. model is great for a 1-2 person household, while 5.0–7.0 cu. ft. is sufficient for 3-4 people. For 5+ households, a dryer with 7.0+ cu. ft. can fit large loads as well as bulky items.

### Are GE Dryers energy efficient?

GE has a wide selection of ENERGY STAR® certified dryers that meet the strict guidelines set by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). They use less energy than standard models, which is great for the environment and your utility bills.

### Can I stack my dryer with a washer?

Yes, you can stack a dryer and washer, provided that they are compatible and front-loading. If you need an appliance to match your stackable dryer, check out our stackable washers.
