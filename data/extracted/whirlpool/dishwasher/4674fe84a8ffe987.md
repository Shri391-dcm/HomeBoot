# Yellow/Brown Discoloration of Plastics

## Possible Solution

### Is the water turned on?

The water supply must be completely open to wash and rinse the dishes properly.

### Why are my plastics yellow or brown?

Yellow/brown discoloration is typically the result of either iron contamination or normal aging of certain plastics. Have the water tested for iron content and correct it if necessary. Unfortunately, plastic discoloration in this manner is practically impossible to remove.
