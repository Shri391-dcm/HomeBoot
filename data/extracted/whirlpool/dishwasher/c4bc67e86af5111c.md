# Warping/Distortion of Plastics

## Possible Solution

### Is the water turned on?

The water supply must be completely open to wash and rinse the dishes properly.

### Was a heated dry cycle used?

Heated-dry cycles can cause temperatures much higher than even some dishwasher-safe plastics may not be able to tolerate. Consider using the air-dry option or loading items in the uppermost racks and away from the heating element.

### Was a high-temperature cycle used?

Some cycle and dry options can drive final rinse temperatures to over 150F, which can cause some plastics to warp or misshapen.

Review How Water Temperature Affects Cycle Time for more information.

### Were the items loaded correctly?

Don't force plastic items into crowded areas or allow heavy objects to rest on them. As the plastic is heated, it can become softer and deform by the forces acting on it.

Review Using Dishwasher Features for Improved Loading for more suggestions.
