# Tarnishing of Cooking Utensils

## Possible Solution

### Why are the cooking utensils tarnishing?

Silver cutlery can become discolored yellow or brown and, in extreme cases, may even turn black. This can be caused by the following:

- Chlorides or sulfides are in the water, possibly from food (salt, eggs, mustard, etc.)
- Metal interaction from silver touching stainless steel
- Natural oxidation

### Resolution

Once tarnished, silver can be restored by using special cleaners such as silver polish. Prevent the problem by keeping silver items away from other types of metals and minimize the number of food soils containing salt, mustard, and eggs. Keep silver stored in a closed box to minimize oxidation in air.
