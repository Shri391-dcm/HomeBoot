# Spotting or Filming on Glassware

## Possible Solution

### Is the water turned on?

The water supply must be completely open to wash and rinse the dishes properly.

### Are you using rinse aid?

The use of rinse aid is very important. Using a rinse aid will optimize your drying and wash performance. Your dishwasher is specifically designed to use liquid rinse aid for improved drying performance and controlling the buildup of hard water deposits.

### Are you using the high-temperature wash option?

Always use a high-temperature wash option, as recommended in your Owner's manual. This option raises the main wash temperature to improve cleaning for loads containing tough, baked-on food.

Review How Water Temperature Affects Cycle Time for more information.

### Are you using the heated dry option?

This option dries dishes with heat. This option, with the use of a rinse aid, will provide the best drying performance. Heated Dry will NOT dry plastic articles or remove all the moisture from convex-bottomed items.

### Have you cleaned the dishwasher?

Extremely hard water mineral deposits can cause damage to your dishwasher and make it difficult to achieve good cleaning. A water softener is strongly recommended if your hardness is 15 grains or more.

Use affresh® Dishwasher Cleaner, white vinegar rinse, or other dishwasher cleaning product to remove white spots and film. Depending on the severity of the spots, more than one treatment may be required.

Review Cleaning the Dishwasher Interior for more information.
