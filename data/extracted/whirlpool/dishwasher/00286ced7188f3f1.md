# Damage to Metallic Decorations on Dishes

## Possible Solution

### Is the water turned on?

The water supply must be completely open to wash and rinse the dishes properly.

### Why is there damage to my metallic dishes?

Dishwasher chemistry and temperature generally do not impact metallic decorations like gold/silver rims. Most often the problem is associated with mechanical wear and tear from contact from handling.

Try to prevent any metallic surfaces from touching during loading/unloading or while in the racks. Consider placing cups on top of the rack tines to avoid rims from rubbing against the rack. Take care not to slide cups on a cupboard or drawer bottoms when storing because once the metallic decoration has been damaged or removed, it cannot be replaced.
