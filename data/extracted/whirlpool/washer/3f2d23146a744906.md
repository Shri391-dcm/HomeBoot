# Lint on Clothing

## Possible Solutions

### Did you line-dry a load of laundry?

When line drying, you can expect some lint on your laundered items. The dryer's air movement and tumbling remove lint from the laundry load.

### Were there new clothing items in the load of laundry?

New clothes tend to create more lint in the first few washes. Washing new clothing inside out will help reduce linting in the cycle.

### Was the load properly sorted?

Separate lint-takers from lint-givers. Synthetics, knits, and corduroy fabrics will pick up lint from towels, rugs, and chenille fabrics.
