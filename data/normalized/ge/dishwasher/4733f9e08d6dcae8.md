# Dishwasher - Use of Drain Cleaner

Should you experience a concern with your dishwasher not draining, it is possible that you may have a clog in the sump area, pump, or drain line/hose. You may want to check out our Dishwasher - Not Draining article.

## Using a Drain Cleaner In a Dishwasher

Chemical drain cleaners such as Drain-O or Liquid Plumber should never be used in a dishwasher for any reason. These types of cleaners are very caustic and can damage the rubber parts inside of the dishwasher.
