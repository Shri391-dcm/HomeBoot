## Bodewell Care Protection Plans FAQs

#### What is a Bodewell Care Plan?

A Bodewell Care Plan is an extended service contract for GE Appliances consumers that helps extend the life of major appliances and keep them operating at peak performance. It's a smart option for homeowners who want added protection, ongoing appliance support, and help caring for their GE Appliances over time.

#### What is a Bodewell Membership?

A Bodewell Membership is a homeowner support membership that offers access to services, savings, and resources for appliance care and home support. It's designed to make it easier to manage your home, maintain important appliances, and get added value from ongoing membership benefits.

#### What is the difference between a Bodewell Care Plan and Membership?

A Bodewell Care Plan helps protect and support major appliances with extended service coverage, while a Bodewell Membership provides broader homeowner benefits, such as services, savings, and ongoing support. In short, a care plan is appliance-focused, and a membership is designed for overall home support.
