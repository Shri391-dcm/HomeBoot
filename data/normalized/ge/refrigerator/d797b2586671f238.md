# WHAT IS R600A REFRIGERANT?

R600a is an HFC-free refrigerant with no ozone depletion potential and a lower global warming potential compared to earlier refrigerants. GE Appliances is committed to reducing the environmental impacts of our business and products. By January 2022, all household refrigerators (except for larger built-in refrigerators and freezers) will be required by regulations to use lower global warming refrigerants. Built-in refrigerators and freezers will transition before January 1, 2023.

GE Appliances, a Haier company has been transitioning to environmentally friendly refrigerants in household refrigerators and freezers. We are proud to offer consumers several models using R600a refrigerant. To assist consumers in their purchasing decisions, we publish the available refrigerator and freezer models already using R600a.
