## Refrigerator Frequently Asked Questions

#### How do I clean and maintain my GE refrigerator?

To keep your GE refrigerator looking great and running efficiently, it's important to clean it regularly and perform a few simple maintenance steps. To clean, wipe down the interior shelves and bins with a soft cloth or sponge using mild detergent and warm water. Then, clean the exterior with a soft cloth and a mild soap (i.e., dish soap) and water mixture. If your refrigerator is made of stainless steel, you can also use Cerama Bryte Stainless Steel Cleaner. Routine maintenances should include checking and cleaning the door gaskets to ensure a tight seal, replacing the water filter as recommended, and vacuuming the condenser coils (if accessible) to help maintain proper airflow. Always refer to your owner's manual for model-specific care instructions.

#### How much space do GE refrigerators come in, and how do I measure for fit?

GE refrigerators are available in a wide range of sizes and capacities to fit different kitchens, from compact models to full-size options for larger households. You'll find each refrigerator's dimensions on its product page. To ensure proper fit, measure the width, height, and depth of your space, including clearance for doors, drawers, and ventilation, then compare that to the dimensions of the refrigerator you choose. Don't forget to account for door swing and space needed to remove shelves or bins. Measuring carefully helps ensure your new refrigerator fits comfortably and operates as designed.

#### What types and styles of GE refrigerators are available?

GE offers a variety of refrigerator styles to suit different needs and kitchen layouts. Options include top-freezer refrigerators, bottom-freezer refrigerators, side-by-side refrigerators, and French-door refrigerators, as well as compact refrigerators and specialty models. Many styles are available with features like adjustable shelving, external or internal water dispensers, ice makers, and smart connectivity, depending on the model.

#### Are GE refrigerators energy efficient?

Yes. Many GE Appliances refrigerators are designed with energy efficiency in mind and meet or exceed ENERGY STAR® guidelines. Energy-efficient features can help reduce electricity usage while still delivering reliable cooling performance. To compare efficiency, look for the ENERGY STAR label and review the Energy Guide on each model for estimated annual energy use.
