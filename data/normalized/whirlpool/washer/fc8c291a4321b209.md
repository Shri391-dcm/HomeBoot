# Cleaning the Dispenser - Washer

## Cleaning the Dispenser Drawer

The dispenser drawer should be removed and cleaned regularly per directions in your Owner's Manual.

- Pull the drawer out until you feel resistance
- Push the two tabs downward, and then continue pulling out.
- Wash in warm, soapy water using a mild detergent.
- Rinse with warm water
- Air dry, or dry with a towel, then place back into the slot.
