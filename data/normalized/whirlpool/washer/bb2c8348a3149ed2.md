# Dry Spots on Clothes After Cycle

## Possible Solutions

### Clothing has some dry spots after the cycle is complete

High-speed spins extract more moisture than traditional top-load washers.

The high spin speeds combined with airflow during the final spin can cause items near the top of the load to develop dry spots during the final spin. This is normal.

NOTE: Do not overload the washer or tightly pack the wash load. If clothing is too tightly packed, some spots could remain dry, and clothing results may not be satisfactory. Consult your Owner's Manual for more information on proper loading, or click the link below.
