# Continuously Fills / Fills and Drains - Washer

## Possible Solutions

### Does the Washer Fill Continuously?

The Drain hose should not extend more than 4.5" (113 mm) into the drainpipe.

The drain hose may be too far down inside the drainpipe (standpipe) or laundry tub. If the drain hose extends too far, drained water can siphon back into the washer. Using the U-shape drain hose form (as described in the Installation Instructions) will provide the correct length of drain hose in the drainpipe or laundry tub.

### Is the Drain installed correctly?

Do not seal the drain hose into the drainpipe with tape. The drain hose should be loose yet secure. A gap between the drain hose and the drain pipe must exist to allow airflow. The air gap will break the suction. This will prevent water from continuously being sucked out of the machine (siphoning) as it fills.

### Is the Drainpipe (standpipe) less than the minimum height of 30"?

Check that the top of the drain is at least 30" (76.2 cm) high (as measured from the floor.) If the drain is below 30" (76.2 cm), the water will start to flow out of the machine due to gravity. The flow of water can create suction (siphon) and the water will continue to drain out of the washer.
