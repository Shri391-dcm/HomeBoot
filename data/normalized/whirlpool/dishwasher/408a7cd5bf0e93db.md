# Dark Marking on Dishes

## Possible Solution

### Why does my dishes have dark marks?

Dark markings on light colored dishes are usually the result of metal being transferred into micro-cracks and pores of the glazing. This is common when spoons are used to stir in cups, or a hand mixer is used in ceramic mixing bowls. Marking can only be prevented by not scraping ceramics with metal items.
