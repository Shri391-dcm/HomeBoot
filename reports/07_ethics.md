# Ethics and Responsible AI

## Purpose

This document outlines the ethical considerations for the Home Appliance AI Support Assistant.

---

## Ethical Principles

The chatbot is designed to provide accurate, transparent, and responsible assistance by using only official Whirlpool and GE Appliances documentation.

---

## Responsible AI Practices

- Retrieve information only from trusted official sources.
- Include source citations in responses whenever possible.
- Avoid generating unsupported or fabricated information.
- Inform users when sufficient information is unavailable.
- Protect user privacy by not collecting unnecessary personal information.

---

## Limitations

- The chatbot may not answer questions outside the supported appliance categories.
- Responses depend on the availability and quality of official documentation.
- The system is intended to assist users and should not replace professional repair services when safety is involved.

---

## Future Improvements

Future versions of the chatbot may include additional appliance categories, improved retrieval accuracy, and enhanced evaluation methods while continuing to follow responsible AI principles.
